The platform

Everything the journey needs

From the first mentor search to a signed offer, each step has a home inside CareerBridge.

Mentor discovery & booking

Browse mentor profiles by expertise, see live availability, and book the slot that fits — then manage every appointment in one view.

Powered by OpenAI

AI résumé analysis

Get an ATS compatibility score and keyword optimization in seconds, so your résumé reads the way recruiters — and their screening tools — expect.

Peer mock interviews

Practice with peers in structured mock sessions and walk away with real-time feedback you can act on before the real thing.

Real-time chat

Message mentors directly over a WebSocket-backed chat — questions, follow-ups, and feedback without leaving the platform.

Secure payments

Pay for sessions through Stripe checkout, with every payment bound to a specific appointment so nothing ends up orphaned.

Role-based access

Students, mentors, staff, and admins each see exactly what they should — governed by feature flags and role-based access control.

How it works

Three steps across the span

The path is short on purpose. Find the right person, lock the time, show up ready.

01

Find your mentor

Filter mentor profiles by expertise and availability until you find the person who's already walked your path.

02

Book and pay

Reserve a time slot and check out securely through Stripe. Your payment and appointment stay linked end to end.

03

Meet and improve

Join the session, chat in real time, and pair it with AI résumé feedback and peer mock interviews between meetings.
